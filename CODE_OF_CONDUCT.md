# Code of Conduct

SimNADES follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience
level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing scientific and technical opinions.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas, methods, evidence, and code.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them when needed.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted
attention, or publication of private information without permission are not
acceptable in this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations
may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
